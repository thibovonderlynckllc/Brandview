# 📝 Draft Preview Guide for Brandview

## 🎯 **What is Draft Preview?**

Draft Preview lets you see changes to your website **before** they go live. Think of it as a "sneak peek" that only you can see.

## 🔍 **How to Use Draft Preview:**

### Step 1: Make Changes

1. Go to your CMS admin: `localhost:3000/admin`
2. Edit any page (Home, About, Services, etc.)
3. Make your changes
4. Click **"Save Draft"** (NOT "Save" - that makes it live immediately)

### Step 2: Preview Your Changes

1. Click the **"Preview"** button
2. You'll see a **RED WARNING BANNER** at the top
3. This shows your draft changes

### Step 3: Publish When Ready

1. Go back to the CMS
2. Click **"Save"** or **"Publish"** to make changes live
3. The red banner will disappear for everyone

## ⚠️ **IMPORTANT: Understanding the Red Banner**

### When You See the Red Banner:

- ✅ **You are viewing DRAFT content**
- ✅ **This is NOT live on your website yet**
- ✅ **Only YOU can see these changes**
- ✅ **Website visitors see the old version**

### When There's No Red Banner:

- ✅ **You are viewing the LIVE website**
- ✅ **This is what everyone sees**
- ✅ **Changes have been published**

## 🚪 **How to Exit Preview Mode:**

**Option 1:** Click "Exit Preview Mode" in the red banner
**Option 2:** Close your browser and reopen the website
**Option 3:** Visit your website in a different browser (Safari, Firefox, etc.)

## 🌍 **What Your Website Visitors See:**

- ❌ **Never see the red banner**
- ❌ **Never see draft content**
- ✅ **Always see the published version**
- ✅ **Normal website experience**

## 🔒 **Privacy & Security:**

- Only people with CMS access can see drafts
- Each browser session is separate
- Your website visitors are completely unaffected
- Draft previews require special authentication

## 💡 **Pro Tips:**

1. **Test in Multiple Browsers:** Preview in Chrome, check live site in Safari
2. **Share Previews Carefully:** Only share preview links with trusted people
3. **Always Double-Check:** Look for the red banner to know if you're in preview mode
4. **Mobile Testing:** Preview mode works on mobile browsers too
